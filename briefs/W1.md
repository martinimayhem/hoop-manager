## Why a version 2

A first W1 was built on branch `world-w1` (commit 0d7a267) against the **old** design: 128 clubs,
two identical pyramids, dollars and euros, a draft in every division, tabs. The owner has decided
that the design in SCOPE.md / GAME_FLOW.md / WORLD_SPEC.md (as merged in PR #3) is the one that
holds. Much of `world-w1` is good and is reused; the world's **shape** and **money** change.

## What W1 delivers

You can start a career, **create the whole world**, pick any club, **look around** (competitions,
clubs, squads, players), **save, load** and see the payroll or budget of any club. No matches,
seasons, signings or scouting yet.

## 2. The world's shape (WORLD_SPEC §1)

| Group | Clubs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Big League, America conference | 18 | Cap system |
| US Divisions 2, 3, 4 | 16 each (48) | Budget system |
| National leagues, 10 countries (ESP ITA FRA GER GRE TUR ISR LTU SRB NED) | 2 tiers × 12 each (240) | Budget system |
| Portuguese League | 12, one tier | Budget system |
| **Total** | **318** | |

- **Big League, Europe conference: 12 clubs that are also in their national tier 1.** 10 hold a
  **licence** (Spain 2, Türkiye 2, Greece, Italy, France, Germany, Israel, Lithuania 1 each) and 2
  hold a **wildcard** for this season. In the generated world, give the wildcards to the two
  strongest non-licence clubs, from two different countries. Store on the club: `licence:
  'licence' | 'wildcard' | null`, and a `bottomTwoFinishes` counter starting at 0.
- **A club belongs to competitions**, not to one tier. Model a `Competition` (`id`, `kind`:
  `bigLeague` / `usDivision` / `nationalLeague`, `region`, `country?`, `tier`, `clubs`). A European
  Big League club is in two. Cups come in W2; leave room for `kind: 'cup'`.
- **Places:** real cities, fictional nicknames, the existing blocklist test. Big cities may host two
  clubs (different nicknames); Lithuania, Israel, Serbia and the Netherlands will need it. Keep the
  `world-w1` American city list where it fits (66 needed), and keep its rule that bigger and more
  famous basketball cities start higher.

## 3. People (WORLD_SPEC §1.4, GAME_FLOW §5.1)

- **13 per first team**, **0–5 in a development squad** (start with 2–3 per club), **6 per academy**
  (European clubs only, 15–18), **~400 free agents**, **40 colleges × 15**. About 6,650 people.
- Nationalities: American clubs mostly American; European clubs mostly from their own country, some
  from elsewhere in Europe and some Americans. Name families for all 11 countries plus the usual
  international ones. Reuse the `world-w1` name generator and blocklist.
- The **102 stars**: spread over both Big League conferences so the conferences are about as strong
  as each other, as in `world-w1`'s test.
- Fields per player as in `world-w1`'s `WorldPlayer`, plus a **promises** list (empty in W1) and
  **morale** (start "okay"). Ages, potential and ceilings as in `world-w1`.

## 4. Money (GAME_FLOW §4): dollars only

- **One currency: dollars.** Remove euros and exchange rates.
- **`moneySystem: 'cap' | 'budget'`** on every club: `cap` for the 18 America Big League clubs,
  `budget` for everyone else.
- **Cap clubs:** cap **C = $100M** (one constant, tunable). Each starts with a payroll between the
  floor (0.9 C) and the tax line (1.21 C), never above the hard ceiling (1.34 C). Contracts 1–5
  years, max 0.30 C a year, minimum 0.01 C. Store each club's **mid-level exception** amount left
  (0.085 C).
- **Budget clubs:** one **club budget** per season (wages, staff, buyouts), by tier as in WORLD_SPEC
  §10.1 (*tune*). Squad wages start at 75–90% of it. Contracts 1–3 years, **each with a buyout fee**
  (higher for better and younger players; lower at smaller clubs).
- **European Big League clubs:** also compute the **threshold** figure: all wages except their 3
  highest-paid (the anchor players) and players under 23, against 0.7 C. Nobody is generated above
  it; in W1 it is shown only.
- Dev-squad players: minimum wage, outside the cap and the threshold.

## 5. Saves and speed

- Reuse `world-w1`'s IndexedDB saves (compressed, versioned, 3 slots). This is a new format: start
  again at version 1. `world-w1` saves never shipped, so they don't need to load.
- Generate in the Web Worker. **Measure** how long creating the world takes in Node and in the
  browser, and report both. It must stay well under 5 s in the browser.

## 6. Screens (GAME_FLOW §6; the owner's mockups, round 1)

The look is the mockups', made from the game's existing colours (`styles.css`: background
`#0e1420`, panels `#172033` / `#1f2a42`, lines `#2c3a58`, text `#e8edf7`, muted `#8d9ab5`, accent
`#ff7a1a`; headings and big numbers in a condensed face such as Barlow Condensed, body text Inter
or the system font).

- **The shell:** a **left sidebar** (Home, Squad, Tactics, Schedule, Competitions, Transfers,
  Scouting, Club, Manager) that collapses to icons below about 1024 px wide; a **top bar** with the
  club badge (initials in the club colours), club name, its competitions and position, the money
  summary (cap space or budget room) and a **Continue** button. In W1, Continue and the sidebar
  items with nothing to show yet (Home, Tactics, Schedule, Transfers, Scouting, Manager) are
  disabled, with a short "Coming soon" tooltip.
- **New career:** choose any club: filter by region and country, then competition; each club shows
  its strength, budget or payroll and its best players. Your name as manager. Then the world is
  created and saved.
- **Squad:** tabs **First team / Dev squad / Academy** (academy only for European clubs), and the
  **view switch Overview / Attributes / Profile** exactly as in GAME_FLOW §6.4 (the Season stats
  view comes in W2), with **stars, morale faces and portraits** as in GAME_FLOW §6.5. Clicking a
  name opens the player page.
- **Player page:** header (large **portrait**, name, number, position, age, nationality, height and
  weight, archetype, origin, morale face), **ability and potential stars** with OVR and the potential
  range next to them (GAME_FLOW §6.5), the attributes in 5 groups with bars, personality,
  contract (wage, years, buyout or rookie scale). No actions in W1.
- **Competitions:** every competition in the world, grouped by region and country. Each opens its
  table (all clubs, 0–0 before the season), with licence/wildcard marks in the Big League Europe.
  Clicking a club opens a read-only club page with its squad.
- **Club:** for your club: the **payroll bar** (GAME_FLOW §4.4): floor, cap, tax line and hard
  ceiling for cap clubs; budget and, for European Big League clubs, the threshold for budget clubs.
  Committed money filled in.
- **Tablet:** every screen works by touch at 768 px wide and up; no information only on hover; tap
  targets at least 44 px.

## 7. Out of W1

Matches, schedules, the season, cups, morale changes, injuries, transfers, the draft, staff,
sponsors, the board, news, the inbox's content. The Quick League and the Friendly match stay as
they are.

## 8. Done when

- `npm run typecheck` clean; `npm test` passes, with `tests/world.test.ts` rewritten for this
  shape. At least these, as WHEN/THEN tests:
  - 318 clubs: 18 + 48 in America, 12 per national tier (10 countries × 2) and 12 in Portugal; every
    club in exactly one league, and the 12 Big League Europe clubs also in their national tier 1.
  - 10 licences split as above and 2 wildcards from two different countries.
  - Every first team has 13, with at least two guards, wings and bigs; dev squads 0–5; academies 6
    and only at European clubs.
  - Every cap club's payroll is between floor and tax line; every budget club's wages are within
    its budget; every budget contract has a buyout fee; every European Big League club is under
    the threshold.
  - Names unique, none on the blocklist; no real club nickname; same seed gives the same world.
  - Save and load give back the same world; a newer or broken save is reported, not loaded.
- `npm run lab` output **identical** to main's (the engine is not touched).
