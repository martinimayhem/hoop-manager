> those files disagree, or something isn't covered: **stop and ask** (CLAUDE.md).

## What E1 delivers

Players get the **21 attributes of SPEC.md §3.1**, and the match engine is reworked so that **every
attribute decides real events and a season's box scores prove it**. A lockdown defender, a ball hawk,
a rim protector, a glass cleaner and a post scorer play differently and end up with different stats.
The lopsided blowouts seen in W2b are fixed on the way. No new career features; the engine,
the data and the screens that show attributes change.

Owner's rule for this slice: **an attribute that does nothing, or does something you can't see in
the stats, is a bug.**

## 1. The attribute model

- `Attributes` (`src/engine/types.ts`) holds exactly the 21 attributes of SPEC.md §3.1 plus
  `injuryProneness` and `potential`. `rebounding`, `dribbling` and `tactics` go (split as in §3.1).
- **Composites** (`src/engine/ratings.ts`) are rewritten. Requirements:
  - every attribute feeds **at least one** composite, and every composite is used by at least one
    engine event;
  - weight no longer stands in for strength, nor height for jumping; height and weight still count
    where size matters (rebounds, rim protection, post-ups);
  - separate composites at least for: perimeter contest, rim contest, steal, block, defensive
    rebound, offensive rebound, post scoring, ball security, shot selection, help defence;
  - a short comment per composite with its formula and the events that use it. Add the same table
    to `docs/engine-attributes.md` (new) so the design chat can review it.
- **Events** (`src/engine/possession.ts` and friends) use the new composites as SPEC.md §3.1 lists,
  including **the counters**: steals raise reach fouls; blocks raise shooting fouls and the chance
  the blocker is out of rebounding position; offensive-rebound crashing leaves fewer men back in
  transition; strength slows a player in transition; post-ups draw double teams.
- **Clutch:** only in the last 5 minutes of the 4th quarter or overtime with the margin at 6 points
  or less; a make-rate change of at most ±3 percentage points at CLU 1 and 99, zero at 50.
- **Offensive IQ / Defensive IQ** take over everything `tactics` did (e.g. `src/engine/tactics.ts`
  "calm", foul rates), split by side of the ball.
- **Overall** (display only, never used by the engine) is recomputed per position from the new
  composites, so that a player's best position (Player page "Position fit") makes sense.

## 2. The data

- **The 102 stars** (`src/data/players.json`): convert their ratings to the 21 attributes with a
  written mapping (old attribute, height, weight and archetype → new ones), so each star keeps his
  style (a "Rim Protector" gets high BLK, INT, JMP; a "Floor General" high PAS, HDL, OIQ). Then write
  **`docs/stars-e1.csv`** (name, position, archetype, old overall, new overall, all 21) and **stop**:
  the owner and the design chat review it before you go on with §4. Expect a list of corrections.
- **The generator** (`src/game/world/generate.ts` and the Quick League pool): every archetype gets a
  coherent profile over the 21 attributes, with spread, so that no two players of one archetype look
  the same. Report the archetype profiles in `docs/engine-attributes.md`.
- **Old saves** convert on load with the same mapping, once (save format version up). An old save
  loads, plays on, and saves in the new format.
- **Everything else that reads attributes** follows: scouting ranges (`src/game/scouting.ts`
  `ATTRIBUTE_GROUPS`: the 5 groups of SPEC.md §3.1), the AI's squad and rotation choices
  (`src/game/ai.ts`), the assistant's tips (`src/game/assistant.ts`), clutch play-calls
  (`src/game/clutch.ts`), the plan editor, the club picker. Search the code for every use of
  `attrs.`.

## 3. Lopsided games

Find why some games end with 40+ point margins (one seen in W2b testing) and fix the cause in the
engine. Target in SPEC.md §3.1b: games decided by 30+ points stay below 5% in the Big League.
Report the cause in one paragraph.

## 4. The checks (SPEC.md §3.1b)

Add to `npm run lab` (or a new `npm run attributes-lab`, same output style):

- **Attribute → stat correlations:** a table with one line per pair of SPEC.md §3.1b, the rank
  correlation over players with at least 15 minutes a game across a simulated season of the Big
  League (both conferences) and two national leagues, and PASS/FAIL against 0.6. Run it with three
  different seeds.
- **Totals by rule set:** a table of the per-team-per-game numbers against the NBA and FIBA ranges.
  Before fixing the ranges, look up the latest full NBA and EuroLeague season averages and report
  them next to ours (with the source) so the design chat can confirm or adjust the ranges.
- **Margins:** share of games decided by 30+, 20+ and 10+ points.
- **The existing tactics check stays:** no tactic wins more than about 60% against the others.
- Make the correlation and totals checks part of `npm test` (a shorter run is fine), so a later
  change can't silently break them.

## 5. Screens

Within the screen rules (nothing scrolls, 14 px, Back in place):

- **Player page:** the attribute panel shows the 21 attributes in the 5 groups of SPEC.md §3.1, in
  two columns (Physical, Scoring, Playmaking | Defence & boards, Mental), each row label, bar and
  number coloured by rating band, as on the reference sheet `design/reference-v1.html` (Player
  profile, "Attributes" panel). Other clubs' players keep showing scout ranges. The rest of the
  reference sheet's new look is **not** part of E1 (it comes with the UI slice).
- **Squad, Attributes view:** the group switch of GAME_FLOW §6.4 (Offence / Defence / Physical &
  mental), short headers as in SPEC.md §3.1; fits 768 px.
- **Match screens:** box scores already show steals, blocks and offensive rebounds; check they
  still add up.

## 6. Done when

- `npm run typecheck` clean; `npm test` passes, with WHEN/THEN tests at least for:
  - every attribute: raising it (all else equal, many possessions) moves its own stat the right way
    (e.g. STL 40 → 80 gives more steals and more reach fouls);
  - each counter of SPEC.md §3.1 shows (more fouls for high-STL and high-BLK defenders, fewer men
    back after offensive-rebound crashes);
  - Clutch does nothing outside its window and at most ±3 points inside it;
  - old saves convert, load and play on; the same seed gives the same game.
- The attribute lab passes on three seeds: every correlation at least 0.6, totals in range for both
  rule sets, 30+ point games below 5%. Report the three tables.
- `docs/stars-e1.csv` reviewed and corrected after the owner's feedback (you stop once, at §2).
- `docs/engine-attributes.md`: composites with formulas and events, archetype profiles.
- `npm run realism` and the realism and man-to-man tests still pass (the animation may need no
  change; results will differ from main, which is expected in this slice).
- No-scroll and navigation sweeps pass; screenshots of the player page and the squad Attributes view
  at 1366×768 and 768×1024 checked by eye.
- **Commit and push** `engine-e1` and report. No pull request: the owner plays it first.

## Stop and ask

- If an attribute can't be given a job you can see in the stats, or two attributes end up doing the
  same thing: stop and ask before dropping or merging one.
- If a correlation can't reach 0.6 without making the game feel wrong (e.g. one stat dominating):
  report the numbers and ask; don't bend the engine to pass the check.
